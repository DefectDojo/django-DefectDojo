# Home

This is my fork of [DefectDojo](https://github.com/DefectDojo/django-DefectDojo), a Django-based
application-security and vulnerability-management platform. DefectDojo lets AppSec and development
teams import findings from dozens of scanning tools, track them across products and engagements,
and manage their remediation lifecycle in one place.

Coursework for CSCI 360 (Software Architecture, Security, and Testing) lives on the
[Project Outcome Analysis](Project-Outcome-Analysis) page and in the
[`/class-docs`](https://github.com/tlmcguire/django-DefectDojo/tree/master/class-docs) directory
of the repository.
