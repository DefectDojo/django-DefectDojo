**My Work** answers one question: what is waiting on me? It is a per-user view — the same page shows each person their own queue.

Open it from **Dashboards > My Work** in the left sidebar.

## The queues

| Queue | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Assigned to Me** | Findings assigned to you personally. Hidden unless the **Work Assignment** feature is enabled. |
| **My Team's Queue** | Findings owned by a group you belong to. Owners is group-level, so this is your team's work rather than yours specifically. |
| **Awaiting My Review** | Findings where you were asked to review and the review is still open. |
| **Reviews I Requested** | Reviews you started that nobody has cleared yet. |
| **My Risk Acceptances** | Risk Acceptances you own that nobody has handled. Expired ones stay listed until they are dealt with. |

Each queue is a full Findings or Risk Acceptances list, so the usual filtering, sorting and column controls all work, and each tab remembers its own column layout.

Everything is scoped by your permissions — My Work never shows you something you could not otherwise see.

## On a dashboard

The same content is available as a **My Work** widget for [custom dashboards](../PRO__custom_dashboards/), so you can put your queue alongside other metrics. Add the widget and choose which queues to show and how many rows each should carry.

There is also a ready-made **My Work** dashboard template under the **Personal** category, which pairs the widget with SLA burndown and recent activity.

## Why a queue rather than notifications

Notifications can be missed, filtered, or arrive while you are not looking. My Work is the durable version: anything waiting on you is here whether or not a notification reached you.

That is why some notifications can be narrowed without losing work. A reviewer who has [scoped their review-request notifications](/admin/notifications/about_notifications/#narrowing-review-request-notifications-pro) to particular assets still sees every request in **Awaiting My Review** — the notification is suppressed, the work is not.
